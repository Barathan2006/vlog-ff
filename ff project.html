The **Money Heist event in Free Fire (FF)** was a special collaboration between the popular mobile battle royale game **Garena Free Fire** and the famous Spanish TV series **"Money Heist" (La Casa de Papel)**. This event, known as **“Plan Bermuda”**, brought themed content inspired by the series, including character costumes, game modes, and missions. Players could take part in a unique limited-time mode where they had to collect money by activating printing machines, much like the heist scenes in the show. The event featured **special outfits resembling the iconic red jumpsuit and Salvador Dalí mask**, as worn by the characters in the series. It added a thrilling and cinematic experience to the game, blending the excitement of Free Fire with the drama and suspense of Money Heist.
